For Small Businesses

Cebu MSME Shipping Guide

From DTI registration to customs clearance — we guide Cebu's small and medium exporters every step of the way, at no extra charge.

1

Register with DTI

Register with BETP under DTI before shipping commercial goods internationally through formal export channels.

2

Prepare Commercial Invoice

Every international shipment requires a commercial invoice with accurate product descriptions, declared values, and HS codes.

3

Pack & Declare Properly

We assist with FedEx-standard packaging and ensure all items are declared correctly to avoid delays or damage claims.

4

Ship & Track in Real Time

Once shipped, track your package live at every worldwide checkpoint until it reaches your buyer's door.

We Handle the Paperwork

Our team assists with customs forms, export declarations, and commercial invoices — so you focus on growing your business.
